# Business Profiling Wiki

## Purpose

Build a cumulative, evidence-based understanding of business profiling and other NSOs' practices to support discovery for Stats NZ Complex Business Support. This wiki informs discovery; it does not decide the team's future operating model.

## Repository

- `raw/` contains the original sources. Treat them as read-only.
- `wiki/` contains the evolving synthesis. Use `index.md` to navigate and append completed work to `log.md`.

## Working agreements

### Ingesting sources

- Follow the [ingest-source skill](.github/skills/ingest-source/SKILL.md) for ingests. Process only sources the user explicitly selects, one at a time; do not ingest the whole `raw/` directory by default.
- Cite substantive claims with the source filename and page or section. Prefer direct source evidence and mark uncertainty or conflicting evidence.
- Keep source evidence, user notes, interpretation, comparisons, and open questions distinct.
- Preserve `raw/` originals; put synthesized material in `wiki/`. Do not copy confidential business records into the wiki.
- Focus on profiling. Include register material only when it explains a profiling decision, input, output or consequence. Internal Stats NZ material is background unless explicitly selected for ingestion.

### Queries and maintenance

- Start with `wiki/index.md`, then consult relevant wiki pages and source material before answering.
- Link claims to their evidence. Do not present an emerging interpretation or an NSO's practice as a universal rule.
- When updating the wiki, check related pages for contradictions, stale claims, missing links, and useful unanswered questions.
- Keep evidence, synthesis, and possible implications for Stats NZ distinct. Treat the future operating model as an open discovery question unless the user says otherwise.

## Learnings

- Maintain [Profiling approaches](wiki/analyses/profiling-approaches.md) as the cumulative answer across ingests and queries: a provisional list of **General approaches** (no cap) and a **Country approaches** table that always records each documented office's approach. The update procedure is step 6 of the ingest skill.
- Distinguish documented use, historical trials, proposals and analyst comparisons. A shared tool, LCU, contact mode or selection threshold is not evidence that two offices use the same delineation method.
