# What varies across profiling programmes?

For **how the units are actually formed**, see [Profiling approaches and where they have been used](profiling-approaches.md). This page compares programme choices around that work.

**Sources:** the [UN Guidelines](../sources/un-guidelines-on-sbr.md) (2024), [Eurostat's European profiling manual](../sources/european-business-profiling-2020.md) (2020), and [Eurostat LCU Guidelines](../sources/eurostat-lcu-guidelines.md) (2026, preliminary). These sources describe different periods, units and scopes: country cases are not rankings or a model for Stats NZ.

| Decision | Contrasting evidence | Why the distinction matters |
|---|---|---|
| Where to spend a profiler's time | [Canada](../countries/canada.md) starts with critical establishments; [the Netherlands](../countries/netherlands.md) scores groups and accepts nominations; [Australia](../countries/australia.md) describes event-triggered review. | Selection, priority within the queue and frequency of review are separate decisions. A large group need not be structurally difficult. |
| What the profile must resolve | [France](../countries/france.md) needs to recognise autonomous divisions; [Colombia](../countries/colombia.md) identifies group members; [Switzerland](../countries/switzerland.md) needs jobs by workplace. | A confirmed group perimeter, an enterprise delineation and a workplace map answer different questions. One does not validate the others. |
| Which unit fits the output | The [2025 Wiesbaden presentation](../sources/wbg-statistical-units-2025.md), slides 7–10, 13, contrasts establishment-based industry detail with enterprise information available from central accounts or systems. | A useful industry or product breakdown need not be the best unit for financial data. Record what can be collected separately from what a published statistic describes. |
| How to involve the business | [Canada](../countries/canada.md) contacts respondents for gaps left after research; [UK](../countries/united-kingdom.md) desk cases include a call; [Switzerland](../countries/switzerland.md) negotiates a recurring data delivery. | “Desk”, “light” and “intensive” are not comparable measures of contact unless the actual activity is described. |
| Who checks the result | [Indonesia](../countries/indonesia.md) assigns approvals for units and links; [South Africa](../countries/south-africa.md) checks both process and product; [the Netherlands](../countries/netherlands.md) feeds inconsistencies across surveys back to profilers. | Supervisor sign-off, register validation and consistency of published statistics are different tests. |
| How strong the evidence is | [Malaysia](../countries/malaysia.md) documents a system capability; [European profiling](../concepts/european-profiling.md) describes a regional method; the national annexes document particular practices. | A tool, a recommended method and an implemented national workflow are not interchangeable evidence. |
| How teams are organized | The 2020 manual reports distinct register-based, statistical-domain and shared models in [France](../countries/france.md), [Italy](../countries/italy.md), [the Netherlands](../countries/netherlands.md), [Switzerland](../countries/switzerland.md), [Poland](../countries/poland.md), [Romania](../countries/romania.md) and [the UK](../countries/united-kingdom.md). | Placement, staffing and division of profiling from data collection varied across offices; the report offers examples rather than a single ideal structure. |
| How global and national boundaries relate | In [European profiling](../concepts/european-profiling.md), the GDC-country team proposes global enterprises and temporary national projections; partner offices retain authority over final national enterprises. | A global proposal or common group perimeter does not replace national statistical-unit decisions. |

## Two directions between profiling and consistency work

- **From structure to checks:** [Spain](../countries/spain.md) validates provisional enterprises with group management. [Belgium](../countries/belgium.md) uses a perimeter agreed with another institution before testing supply and use. [Poland](../countries/poland.md) stores each year's profiled legal units with its checks. In all three, defining which units a figure covers comes before comparing figures.
- **From checks back to structure:** [Italy](../countries/italy.md) investigated an apparent export discrepancy, confirmed a customer's domestic branch and revised the turnover split and the profile. But [Sweden](../countries/sweden.md) also finds differences due to concepts or survey instructions. A failed check is a reason to investigate, not an instruction to redraw the group.
- **Who acts after a finding:** [Ireland](../countries/ireland.md) edits combined returns before handing them to the domains; [Denmark](../countries/denmark.md) agrees and follows up corrections that domains implement. Compare mandates, not just organisational charts. ([Eurostat](../sources/eurostat-lcu-guidelines.md), §§2.1, 3.2, 6.1–6.8.)

**Questions for later sources.** How does an office distinguish a real structural event from a new discovery about the old structure? Which measure shows that a profile improved collection or estimates, rather than merely that it was completed? Who can approve a revised reporting boundary, and how are users of earlier periods informed? These are discovery questions, not recommendations.

**Historical caveat.** The 2020 manual's staffing, population and organizational figures are contemporaneous snapshots, not current targets. Some narrative and table counts differ (including for France, the Netherlands and the UK); those differences remain unresolved in the source note and country pages.
