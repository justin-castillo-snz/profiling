Start with [Profiling a complex business](concepts/profiling-workflow.md) for what profilers do, or [What varies across programmes?](analyses/profiling-practices.md) to compare offices. Unfamiliar term? See the [glossary](concepts/glossary.md). Country accounts are dated evidence, not necessarily current policy.

## Sources
- [UN Guidtical Business Registers](sources/un-guidelines-on-sbr.md) — source scope, reading copy and a question-by-question guide to the relevant chapters and annexes.
- [Eurostat Guidelines for Large Cases Units](sources/eurostat-lcu-guidelines.md) — preliminary 2026 edition; how group profiles support cross-survey checks, and what discrepancies reveal about group structure.
- [Eurostat European business profiling manual](sources/european-business-profiling-2020.md) — 2020 recommendations on the collaborative European method, enterprise delineation, casework, contact and NSI organization.
- [Wiesbaden Group: Statistical Units](sources/wbg-statistical-units-2025.md) — 2025 discussion of enterprise versus establishment, ISIC classification of multinationals and comparability across profiling methods.
- [Wiesbaden Group: Stats SA profiling processes, abstract](sources/wbg-stats-sa-profiling-abstract-2025.md) — 2025 abstract on enterprise-group profiling, its challenges and the exclusion of foreign companies.
- [Wiesbaden Group: Stats SA profiling processes, presentation](sources/wbg-stats-sa-profiling-presentation-2025.md) — 2025 slides on scope, four-step process, selection threshold, duration and group-level classification.
- [Statistics Canada EPM Program (Voorburg Group, 2018)](sources/voorburg-statcan-epm-2018.md) — 2018 self-description of 300-enterprise portfolio management: selection, interviews, collection, coherence and training.
- [Statistics Canada EPM deck for NZ LCU meeting (2025)](sources/statcan-epm-nz-lcu-meeting-2025.md) — slide deck: 230-enterprise portfolio, selection, legal basis, relationship, profiling, collection and coherence roles, hiring; NZ's open questions.
- [Statistics Canada IBSP Overview (2015, historical)](sources/statcan-ibsp-overview-2015.md) — survey-platform paper: BR as frame, four statistical units, allocation factors, smart replacement, 2015 EPM context.
- [Wiesbaden Group: The Spanish LCU, paper and abstract](sources/wbg-ine-spain-lcu-paper-2025.md) — 2025 INE account of LCU design, two-stage selection, intensive profiling procedure, coverage and the proposed MNEData channel.
- [Wiesbaden Group: The Spanish LCU, presentation](sources/wbg-ine-spain-lcu-presentation-2025.md) — condensed 2025 slides; portfolio labelling differs from the paper.
- [Wiesbaden Group: European experience with LCUs, paper](sources/wbg-eurostat-lcu-paper-2025.md) — Eurostat 2025 overview of LCU purposes, EWS, adoption and draft consistency-check guidelines, with country examples.
- [Wiesbaden Group: European experience with LCUs, presentation](sources/wbg-eurostat-lcu-presentation-2025.md) — slides with the 2024 LCU survey adoption table and takeaways; count dates differ from the paper.
