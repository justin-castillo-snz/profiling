# Wiesbaden Group: European experience with Large Cases Units (presentation)

21 slides presented by Enrica Morganti (Eurostat G.1) for the authors of the [paper](wbg-eurostat-lcu-paper-2025.md), Session 3 ("Globalisation and Profiling of Multinational Enterprises"), 29th Wiesbaden Group meeting, Johannesburg, October 2025. © European Union 2025 (CC BY).

## Evidence guide

| Slides | Content |
|---|---|
| 2–3 | An LCU is an NSI unit that assures the quality of data on large MNE groups, comparing group data with business and tax statistics and annual/financial reports and checking representation in national accounts and balance of payments by the principle of economic ownership. LCUs are voluntary in EU-EFTA, often absent in small countries, placed differently (national accounts versus business/trade statistics), and need organisational change away from stovepipes. |
| 4–5 | Activities: relations with the largest groups, data collection, profiling, EWS (established 2017), consistency checks. Some countries collect group data centrally and redistribute to domains. In 2024, over 20% of the 934 groups followed by national LCUs were also in Eurostat's European profiling programme. |
| 6–7 | 2024 LCU survey (27 EU, 4 EFTA countries). Situation as of March 2025: 18 countries with an LCU, 4 with a similar structure, 4 creating one, 5 with none. Table of start years: SE 2004; IE 2009; FR, FI, NL 2010; IT 2017; DK, LU, NO 2018; GR, RO 2019; LV, LT, DE 2020; BE 2021; ES 2022; AT 2024; HU listed without a year. Planned: BG, PL 2025; CH 2026. Similar structure: SK (2024), CY, SI, PT, EE. None: CZ, HR, MT, LI, IS. |
| 8–10 | Key features, benefits and challenges, as in the paper. |
| 11–16 | Purpose, drafting and contents of the guidelines on MNE group data consistency checks; topics of interest. |
| 17–19 | Denmark: source map and checks (turnover and traded goods compared across business statistics, Prodcom and balance of payments). Belgium: supply-and-use approach. |
| 20 | Takeaways: checks vary with institutional arrangement, production-cycle stage and sources; timing, prioritisation, contacts at the right level, coherence versus consistency, tools. |

## Limitations and discrepancies

- Same authorship as the paper; not independent corroboration.
- The slide table gives IT 2017, DK 2018, LU 2018, whereas the paper says "2017–18" collectively. The slides give Ireland 2009; the [INE presentation](wbg-ine-spain-lcu-presentation-2025.md) groups IE, FR, FI and NL as "2009–10". Start years are as reported by Eurostat, not verified by each office.
- "Operational LCU" is counted as 18 as of April 2024 in the paper and March 2025 here, and the table's 18 includes offices starting through 2024. Unresolved.
- Slides are summary-level; qualifications in the paper may be absent.
