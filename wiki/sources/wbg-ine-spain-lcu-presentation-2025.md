# Wiesbaden Group: The Spanish LCU (presentation)

Iván Pérez-Plaza and Sixto Muriel (INE Spain), 24-slide presentation with the same title as the [paper](wbg-ine-spain-lcu-paper-2025.md), Session 3 (S3.2), 29th Wiesbaden Group meeting, Johannesburg, 27 October 2025.

- **Original:** `WBG - Presentation Session 3 S3.2_INE_Spain-Presentation.pdf`. Obtained from the [Wiesbaden Group CIRCABC library](https://circabc.europa.eu/ui/group/ea13cb47-2205-497e-94db-e812a440b486/library/4ac5d0df-0e83-414f-bfb2-35bb7ac5a0ec).
- **Reading copy:** Markdown conversion with extracted slide images, not published with this wiki.

| By topic (slide numbers not recoverable from the reading copy) | Profiling identifies LEU, ENT and GEG, clarifies control (GGH, UCI), locates the GDC and sets up efficient data-collection structures. Spanish LCU: established 2022, reports to the INE President, coordinates across domains. Workflow: sources are the EGR, business register, administrative data and surveys; first contact from the INE President to the group's President or CEO; intensive profiling leads to delineation, consolidation and integration into the register and EGR; annual follow-up maintains it. Coverage: 2024 portfolio 20 groups, 2.4% of jobs, €246bn turnover; 2025 portfolio 41 groups, 3.2% of jobs, €370bn turnover. Outcomes, challenges, lessons, MNEData benefits, risks and roadmap (approval 2025, pilot 2026, first run 2027–28) and conclusions mirror the paper. Two anonymised illustrative examples are shown and not reproduced here. |

## Limitations

- Same authors and perspective as the paper, so it is not independent corroboration.
- **Labelling ambiguity (unresolved):** the presentation's 2023 → 20 and 2024 → 41 differ from the paper's "2024 portfolio (reference year 2023)" and "2025 portfolio (reference year 2024)". They probably describe the same portfolios, but the sources do not say so.
- Slide titles and charts give less detail than the paper, and the paper's qualifications may be missing.
