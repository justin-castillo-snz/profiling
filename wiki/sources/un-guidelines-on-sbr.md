# United Nations: Guidelines on Statistical Business Registers

United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Statistics Division, *Guidelines on Statistical Business Registers*, New York, 2024. Studies in Methods, Series F No. 122; ST/ESA/STAT/SER.F/122. The original PDF (`UN_Guidelines_on_SBR.pdf`) and the Markdown reading copy used for this ingest are not published with this wiki.

**What this source establishes.** Profiling uses accounts, structural research and, where needed, discussions with a business to identify the units operating within a group and agree a reporting structure. The outcome is not just a description of ownership: it should be possible to collect meaningful statistics without asking the business to report the same activity twice. Profiles need revision as businesses change. The Guidelines describe a common purpose and several implementations, not a single prescribed procedure (§6.6.4, paras 6.120–6.127; Annex F).

**Limits.** The 2024 publication date is not a common observation date for the cases. For instance, Annex C6 recounts work beginning in 2013, and F5 describes both an operating unit and proposals it had not adopted. National terms may not map one-to-one to international units. Use section, paragraph and figure numbers to find evidence in the reading copy: converted tables can lose column alignment. Chapter 12 (§12.2.1) identifies delineation of multinational groups and comparison of national approaches as topics for further work. This ingest covers profiling evidence, not the whole field of register design.
