# Colombia

## Building and validating a domestic group perimeter

[UN Guidelines](../sources/un-guidelines-on-sbr.md), Annex F3, p. 367. Public management reports and other web sources supplied a consolidated evidence base. The team identified legal units linked to holding companies and flagged possible special-purpose entities. It characterised units in the resident or [truncated group](../units/group-and-enterprise.md) while retaining affiliate information for later expansion to the whole group. A recorded affiliate is therefore not necessarily part of a completed worldwide profile.

The case reports **98% accuracy for identifying constituent legal units in the Colombian register**, not for all data fields or all profiling decisions. Visits to two groups were used to present and discuss the results. The annex does not specify the accuracy denominator or establish that every group received a visit. This is evidence for a desk-led perimeter investigation followed by targeted confirmation, not evidence for a national contact frequency.
