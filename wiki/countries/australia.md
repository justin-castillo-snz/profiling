# Australia

## Targeted maintenance rather than profiling every registration

[UN Guidelines](../sources/un-guidelines-on-sbr.md), Box 7.2, pp. 156–157; Box 11.11, pp. 254–255. For the ABS-maintained population, profiling sat alongside the register survey, survey feedback and ad hoc investigation. Size, complexity and propensity to change informed treatment; triggers supported review as real changes occurred. The tax-office-maintained population was described as maintained from tax records. The new ability to include ABS information there was an option, not proof it had already replaced that arrangement. See [case selection and review cycles](../concepts/case-selection.md).

Box 11.11 explains a downstream use: manually collected profiling data and administrative data helped maintain the economic units model underlying quarterly frame snapshots. The proposed move towards an integrating spine was a wider register development. It should not be read as a separate profiling method or proof that the transition was complete.
