# France

INSEE carried out [European top-down profiling](../analyses/profiling-approaches.md#top-down-from-the-group-to-autonomous-enterprises) both as lead and partner office. For national profiling it sought the French enterprises' legal-unit composition, internal flows and consolidated turnover by activity. [Eurostat profiling manual (2020)](../sources/european-business-profiling-2020.md), §6.B.1, pp. 97–98.

## When one legal unit contains several enterprises

[UN Guidelines](../sources/un-guidelines-on-sbr.md), Box 4.4, p. 79. In French public or newly privatised groups, the dominant legal unit could contain distinct autonomous operational divisions. Profiling recognised several enterprises corresponding to those divisions; activities and even institutional sectors could differ. This is not a rule to split every large legal unit. It is evidence that **organisational autonomy can justify a split**, just as a lack of autonomy can justify combining legal units elsewhere (§4.4.2).

See [group, control and enterprise](../units/group-and-enterprise.md) for the distinction and [profiling workflow](../concepts/profiling-workflow.md) for how that decision changes the statistical structure.

## Register support for profiled units

Box 2.7 (pp. 39–40), describing the Sirus register's data integration, lists ProfilApp as the register for enterprises profiled from enterprise groups and LIFI as the register for enterprise groups. Both are among the sources Sirus accepts automatically, while the administrative business register (Sirene) supplies legal units. The box describes the production process, not how profiling itself is conducted. Do not infer from it who profiles, how often or with what thresholds.

## National profiling model reported in 2020

The [Eurostat 2020 manual](../sources/european-business-profiling-2020.md), §6.B.1, pp. 96–98, reports that INSEE manually profiled the largest non-financial groups in France and used an algorithm for other groups. Its prose says 50 groups were manually profiled, while Table 1 reports 55 and a target of 70 within two to three years; the manual does not reconcile these counts. It describes a ten-expert team combining register, enterprise delineation, data collection, SBS and consolidated profit-and-loss/balance-sheet work, with regular seminars and case-specific discussions involving statistical users. A pilot SBS survey for about 70 large groups began in November 2018. These are 2020-era descriptions, not verified current figures or a policy prescription. See [profiling workflow](../concepts/profiling-workflow.md) and [team organization](../concepts/lcu-coordination.md).
