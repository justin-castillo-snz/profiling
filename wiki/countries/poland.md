# Poland

## Preserve the year's group structure with the year's checks

[Eurostat LCU Guidelines](../sources/eurostat-lcu-guidelines.md), §§1.4, 6.8, pp. 73–87. In the April 2024 survey Poland was setting up a unit; fig. 1 dates implementation to 2025. Chapter 6 describes both an implemented database and intended uses. The profiling team supplies the results of manual and automatic profiling. These are stored by year and provide the basis for identifying the groups and constituent legal units in that year's LCU target population; today's perimeter should not be assumed for prior returns. See [case selection](../concepts/case-selection.md).

Survey tables retain source-specific fields alongside common keys. Validation can compare values for a legal unit or an entire group, with separate outcomes for a measured difference, a missing return, a missing code and a zero. Results before and after survey closure can both be retained; analysts can assign a case, record a decision and attach an explanation. The distinction matters: absent data are not evidence of inconsistent reporting, and a failed check need not imply the profile is wrong. (§§6.8.1–6.8.4; [change and quality](../concepts/profile-change-and-quality.md).)

## Profiling-team comparison reported in 2020

**2025 Eurostat overview.** Eurostat's paper lists Poland among examples developing a dedicated IT tool for consistency checks; its slides give 2025 as the planned LCU year. ([Paper](../sources/wbg-eurostat-lcu-paper-2025.md), pp. 9–10; [slides](../sources/wbg-eurostat-lcu-presentation-2025.md), 7.)

The [Eurostat 2020 manual](../sources/european-business-profiling-2020.md), Chapter 6, Table 1, p. 101, describes the Polish model as a temporary volunteer team of 10–15 people in the business-register, SBS and OFATS areas. The table reports 10–15 multinational groups with a Polish GDC and about 10 domestic groups being profiled at the time, with a permanent team and annual treatment of 150 groups planned from 2019. It describes a business-register maintenance approach and an objective of enterprise delineation and core variables. The text is a historical comparison; it does not establish whether the planned permanent team or volume was realized.
