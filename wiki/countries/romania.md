# Romania

## Profiling-team comparison reported in 2020

The [Eurostat 2020 manual](../sources/european-business-profiling-2020.md), Chapter 6, Table 1, p. 101, reports that Romania's profiling scope covered all groups within structural business statistics, about 7,150 groups. It lists economic weight as the selection criterion, six groups manually treated per year and approximately 7,000 treated automatically, annually. The described team had 10 FTE, was located across the business-register and SBS areas, and aimed to delineate enterprises and maintain the population and continuity of enterprises. The table says there was no LCU. This is a dated comparative account; it does not establish current Romanian practice or explain the method behind the counts.

See [case selection](../concepts/case-selection.md) for the distinction between manual and automatic treatment and [profiling-team organization](../concepts/lcu-coordination.md) for the 2020 manual's comparison of NSI models.
