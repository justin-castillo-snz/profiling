# Malaysia

## Profiling capability in an enterprise module

[UN Guidelines](../sources/un-guidelines-on-sbr.md), Annex C5, p. 325, “Business profiling”. The Department of Statistics Malaysia describes profiling as analysing the legal, operational and accounting structure of large enterprises and groups. It created an enterprise module in its integrated statistical systems framework to facilitate that work.

**Limit of the evidence:** this documents investment in a capability. The passage does not specify how many groups were profiled, how they were selected, how often they were contacted, or how the resulting profiles were validated. Use the [profiling workflow](../concepts/profiling-workflow.md) to compare those details when further sources become available, not to fill them in now.
