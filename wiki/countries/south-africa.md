# South Africa

## A group file and two kinds of quality check

[UN Guidelines](../sources/un-guidelines-on-sbr.md), Annex F6, pp. 379–389, figs F6.1–F6.5; Box 7.2. The annex reproduces a quality instrument: its questions show what analysts were asked to check, **not** how often a check passed.

- **Trigger:** For the designated profiling population, administrative changes and survey feedback signal a need to reprofile. Box 7.2 distinguishes this from the improvement surveys and ad hoc investigations used elsewhere in the register. See [case selection](../concepts/case-selection.md).
- **File the evidence:** Associate the profiler and supervisor with the group file. The inventory includes accounts, organisational chart, profile spreadsheet, classification approval, correspondence, interview agenda and outcome, and the analyst's self-assessment. An auditable file contains more than the revised diagram. (F6.1–F6.2.)
- **Trace the process:** Record the initial group, surveys and [observation units](../units/observation-and-reporting.md); initiation; analysis of the current position; interview preparation and follow-up; revised delineation; and dissemination and use. Compare units and classifications before and after. Check that the register, survey-management system and administrative links reflect the agreed result. Ask whether affected survey teams were informed and how they responded. (F6.2–F6.4.)
- **Assess the product:** Review relevance to users, accuracy, timeliness, accessibility, interpretability and coherence. For example, a technically correct classification can still leave survey staff unable to explain a series break. That last example illustrates why the two checks differ; it is not a reported failure. (F6.5; [profile change and quality](../concepts/profile-change-and-quality.md).)
- **Where profiling sits in the register:** Box 6.4 (p. 119) says SBR components are responsible for the annual quality improvement survey, survey-area feedback, VAT investigations and profiling, and that a dedicated team does quality control of the classification of all units those components investigate. Initial classification comes from the revenue authority. The box does not say how large the team is.
