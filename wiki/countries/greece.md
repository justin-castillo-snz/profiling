# Greece

## Staff assigned versus time available

[Eurostat LCU Guidelines](../sources/eurostat-lcu-guidelines.md), §2.3, p. 16: ELSTAT reported nine full-time employees in the unit, with experience spanning structural business statistics, production statistics, methodology and the business register. They also had other responsibilities, so effective capacity for LCU work was substantially smaller. This matters when comparing [casework mandates](../concepts/lcu-coordination.md): nine employees is not evidence of nine full-time profilers. The passage gives no measured profiling FTE.
