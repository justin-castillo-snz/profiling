# Germany

## Dividing responsibility for a large case

[Eurostat LCU Guidelines](../sources/eurostat-lcu-guidelines.md), §2.3, table 1, pp. 18–20. The Federal Statistical Office's role description separates methodology across cases from responsibility for an individual investigation. Account managers coordinate analysis, prepare group contacts, document results and coordinate their use; methodology staff advise on cases and translate between business accounts and statistical concepts. Federal-state coordinators connect the LCU with domain teams in the states and participate in decisions to admit or discontinue cases. IT responsibilities explicitly include confidentiality and retention controls. This is evidence of [decision rights and skills](../concepts/lcu-coordination.md), not a specified German enterprise-delineation rule.
