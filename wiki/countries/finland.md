# Finland

## A decentralised consistency unit

[Eurostat LCU Guidelines](../sources/eurostat-lcu-guidelines.md), §§1.4, 2.1, pp. 10–13, dates Finland's LCU to 2010 and classifies its approach, alongside Denmark and Sweden, as decentralised. In the authors' distinction the LCU facilitates consistency work while individual statistical domains perform much of the data work. The passage does not specify Finland's thresholds, interview procedures or authority over a particular register correction. See [LCU decision rights](../concepts/lcu-coordination.md).
